## Doctrine

Canonical Lane Mathlib packages assert only:

```text
manifold-constrained / admissible-class closure
```

They do **not** assert unrestricted classical conjecture closure.

```text
IN SCOPE:  constrained closure with explicit class, bridge, endpoint, audit, remainder
OUT OF SCOPE: unrestricted classical global theorem closure
FORBIDDEN:  promoting local or constrained success into classical global closure
```

This is the operating law also stated in
[`p-vs-np-canonical-lane`](https://github.com/HautevilleHouse/p-vs-np-canonical-lane).

## Why this is normative, not optional

1. **Search-space / concept-formation:** unrestricted classical formulas often quantify over a class too large for lawful closure. Concepts and claims need a bounded measurement class before closure is assertible.
2. **P vs NP lane:** manifold-constrained admissible-class gates can pass while classical global equality remains carried (`kappa_general` unrealized).
3. **Jacobian \(n \ge 3\):** a classical unrestricted formula already failed (constant nonzero Jacobian / local invertibility does not imply global polynomial invertibility). Packet:
   [commentary/jacobian-c3-counterexample](https://github.com/HautevilleHouse/commentary/tree/main/openconjectures/jacobian-c3-counterexample).
   The plane case \(n=2\) remains open/carried.
4. **BSD and Poincaré packages:** deep constrained routes with classical remainder carried; they are tracked as survivor / differently-handled targets, not as proof that classical packaging is generally safe.
